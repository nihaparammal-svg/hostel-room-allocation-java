About The College Hostel System

The College Hostel Room Allocation and Vacancy Registration System is an automated, secure institutional platform designed to eradicate manual paperwork and favoritism in college residential allocations.

Built exclusively using Java, Spring Boot, Spring Data JPA, and Thymeleaf, the system guarantees 100% data integrity, atomic bed vacancy calculations, and ironclad multi-user data isolation between student and administrative accounts.

Hostel Residency Rules

  • Residency is allocated for one academic year and must be re-applied for subsequent semesters.
  • Bed transfers or subletting are strictly prohibited without written approval from the Chief Warden.
  • Campus curfew is 10:00 PM for all residential wings unless authorized by academic leave permits.
  • All rooms are subject to periodic safety and maintenance inspections by hostel administration.
